Fixi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version

Fixi watches what you type, on your computer, so that it can notice when something was typed in the wrong keyboard language and fix it. That information never leaves your computer, is never written to disk, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Fixi has no internet access of any kind.

What Fixi observes

To do its job, Fixi has to see your keystrokes as you type them. It keeps them in a small area of memory — at most the current word or short phrase — so it can tell whether you meant Hebrew or English.

Fixi also notices when a mouse button is pressed — only that it was pressed, never where, and never how the mouse moves — because a click can move the cursor, and Fixi must then forget what it was tracking.

That memory is cleared:

  • as soon as a correction is made,
  • whenever you move to a different window or text box,
  • whenever you move the cursor, with the keyboard or by clicking,
  • whenever Fixi is paused,
  • the moment a password field is detected.

What Fixi never does

  • It never writes what you type to disk. No log files, no history, no crash dumps containing your text.
  • It never sends anything anywhere. The app declares no network permissions and contains no networking code. This is verifiable: the package manifest lists its capabilities, and no internet capability is requested.

  • It stays out of password fields it can recognise. Fixi asks Windows what kind of control you are typing into. If it is a password field, Fixi stops looking. If Windows cannot answer at all, Fixi assumes the worst and stops looking.

    Some apps do not tell Windows which kind of control is focused — notably apps built on Electron, such as Visual Studio Code, Slack, Microsoft Teams and Discord. In those apps Fixi cannot recognise a password field. What you type there is still held only in memory, and is never stored or sent anywhere, but Fixi may try to correct it. If you would rather Fixi stayed out of such an app entirely, add it under Settings → What to leave alone.

  • It collects no analytics, telemetry or usage statistics, and shows no advertising.

What Fixi stores on your computer

Two things, both local, and both yours to delete at any time:

  1. Your settings — how eager corrections should be, which applications to leave alone, whether to play a sound.
  2. Words you have explicitly corrected — and only if you turn this on. When you undo a correction, or flip a word yourself, Fixi can remember that word so it does not make the same mistake again. This is off until you choose to enable it, it records only words you acted on deliberately, and never words you merely typed. The settings window lists every remembered word and lets you delete them individually or all at once.

Both are stored in the app's own folder on your computer. Uninstalling Fixi removes them.

Purchases

Fixi is sold through the Microsoft Store, including its free trial. The Store handles the purchase and tells Fixi only whether this copy is licensed and whether a trial is still running. We receive no payment details, and Fixi has no account, no sign-in and no user profile.

Children

Fixi is a typing utility with no social features, no content and no data collection. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and the date at the top will change. Any change that would affect what Fixi observes or stores will also be announced in the app itself.
